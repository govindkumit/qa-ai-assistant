LOGIN REQUIREMENTS

1. Users must provide a valid username and password to log in.

2. After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, the user account
   will be locked.

3. The account will remain locked for 15 minutes.

4. A successful login resets the failed login attempt counter.

5. Users can reset their password using the "Forgot Password"
   functionality.

6. Passwords must contain at least 8 characters.

7. Passwords must contain at least one uppercase letter,
   one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character.
